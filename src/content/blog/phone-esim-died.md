I got a Pixel 11 last week and have been turning it into my main phone. I built my own home screen for it and was finally starting to use my iPhone less. Everything worked Friday night.

Saturday morning I picked it up and had no service. I also couldn't connect to my computers from it. I had no idea what was going on.

It turns out the phone installed an update by itself at about 2 in the morning and restarted. After that, no SIM. It's an eSIM, so I thought there was nothing physical to break. I was wrong about that. An eSIM is still a real chip soldered inside the phone. The plan is software, but it has to live somewhere.

I restarted it. Then I powered it all the way off. I toggled airplane mode because support told me to. I reset the network settings. Support had me try to erase the eSIM and I couldn't even do that, because the chip wasn't answering. The logs showed the slot in an error state with a blank ID.

I even switched back to the stock home screen, because I wanted to be sure it wasn't something I built. Same thing.

Support ended up sending me a replacement. I still don't know for sure what happened. It worked Friday night and didn't work Saturday morning, and the update was the only thing in between that I can point to.

Good thing I kept the iPhone. I'm not getting rid of it yet.
